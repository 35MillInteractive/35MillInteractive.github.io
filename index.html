A closer look / Testing practice

The details make the difference.

Player-state & death-prevention testing +

I compared behavior across quick relogs and full character spawns to distinguish a persistent status effect from a newly triggered script. I also confirmed that unconfigured character-sheet rolls return unmodified results, rather than zero.

Sound nodes & camera transitions +

I tested four sound-node types side by side, documenting who hears playback, where it occurs, and which controls take effect. I also identified the camera-position setup needed for smoother transitions after fade-to-black.

Server troubleshooting & validation +

I used separate test and main servers to investigate mod and load-order issues, worked with hosting support to narrow down causes, and validated recovery before changes reached the main server.
