QA Case Studies

Short write-ups of testing work from my Conan Exiles project.

01

Death-Prevention & Player-State Testing

Tested
Death-prevention behavior, plus character-sheet rolls with no configuration.
Method
Repeated the scenario across quick relogs and full character spawns, comparing expected vs. actual results in each case.
Finding
Told a persistent status effect apart from a newly triggered script. Confirmed that unconfigured character-sheet rolls return unmodified results, not zero.
02

Sound-Node & Camera Comparison

Tested
Four sound-node types, plus camera transitions after fade-to-black.
Method
Tested each node type side by side and documented who hears it, where playback occurs, and which controls take effect.
Finding
Documented the behavior of all four node types. Identified the camera-position setup needed to prevent awkward transitions after fade-to-black.
03

Server Load-Order Troubleshooting

Tested
Mod and load-order issues and server startup problems.
Method
Kept separate test and main servers, investigated issues on the test environment, and worked with hosting support to narrow down causes.
Finding
Narrowed down the causes with hosting support and validated recovery before changes reached the main server.
